I am a Ph.D. Student in the Control and Dynamic Systems Lab at the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Seoul National University, under the supervision of Professor Hyungbo Shim.

My research interests include **control system security,** **learning-based control with guarantees, and stochastic optimal control.**
