About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Control and Dynamic Systems Lab at Seoul National University, advised by Professor Hyungbo Shim. My work focuses on secure and privacy-preserving control, learning-based control with guarantees, and stochastic optimal control under uncertainty.

Research areas

Encrypted control, differential privacy, robust decision-making, and data-driven feedback design.
